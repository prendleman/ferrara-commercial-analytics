# Limits to say out loud

- Figures are synthetic. They are not Ferrara results, Ferrero results, or a calibrated company forecast.
- Brand names are public flavor. Accounts and owners are fictional.
- The book is 46 fictional accounts across four regions and seven fiscal years. It is not an enterprise revenue claim.
- Trade rates and margin ratios are generation rules, not a customer P&L.
- Seasonality is a month multiplier on Halloween and December families. It is not a demand-planning model.
- Quarantine is four planted bad invoices, enough to show the gate, not a production quality program.
- Account scope is an application predicate (`account_id = ?`), not a database policy engine.
- The assistant matches phrases. It does not understand paraphrase beyond the listed routes.
- Syndicated retail measurement, SAP IBP, and enterprise MDM are refused because they are outside this model and outside the experience being claimed.
- No messages are sent from this app.
